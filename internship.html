Backend & AI Engineering Intern — Volvo Construction Equipment

During this internship I contributed to an enterprise AI platform effort at Volvo Construction Equipment: a centralized portal hosting multiple AI-powered business applications, a monitoring/observability layer for that platform, and natural-language assistants that help internal users search technical and diagnostic knowledge bases. The summary below intentionally describes the capabilities and engineering patterns involved rather than internal architecture, data sources, or proprietary implementation details.

Django PostgreSQL + pgvector Azure App Service Microsoft Entra ID (MSAL) SSO RAG / Hybrid Search REST APIs Bash / Linux Deployment

AI Marketplace — Enterprise AI Platform

A centralized, enterprise-grade AI platform built to host and deliver multiple AI-powered business applications under a single, secure, unified web portal — enabling different teams across the organization (sales, dealer support, product engineering, finance, market intelligence, and more) to access purpose-built AI tools through one common gateway, without each team needing to build and maintain separate infrastructure.

  • Designed and helped build an enterprise AI marketplace platform that hosts multiple independent AI-powered applications under a single secure portal, enabling centralized access, authentication, and governance across business functions.
  • Contributed to a scalable multi-tenant platform allowing new AI tools/applications to be independently developed and plugged in without disrupting existing services, reducing time-to-deploy for new business use cases.
  • Implemented enterprise single sign-on (SSO) and role-based access control so employees across different departments securely access only the tools relevant to their role.
  • Helped build the platform on a cloud-native architecture (Microsoft Azure) with automated deployment pipelines, supporting reliability, security, and scalability for company-wide use.
  • Contributed to a unified user experience layer (a modern, consistent web interface) across a diverse suite of AI applications spanning customer support, market analytics, forecasting, document intelligence, and operational insights.
  • Supported self-service AI adoption across business units by providing a common platform where new AI capabilities can be onboarded and made available to end users quickly and securely.

Observability & Health Monitoring Dashboard

A companion monitoring dashboard that gives platform administrators visibility into the health, errors, incidents, and usage of the AI marketplace — without generating that data itself. It reads and visualizes operational signals the main platform already produces, so problems can be spotted and triaged quickly.

  • Built a superuser-gated dashboard summarizing service uptime/degradation, open incidents, recent errors, and unread alerts at a glance.
  • Implemented incident grouping so repeated errors of the same type/service become a single trackable incident (open → acknowledged → resolved), instead of a flood of duplicate alerts.
  • Added noise filtering (dropping static-asset/favicon noise and duplicate log entries) and stale-heartbeat detection to flag services as degraded/down when expected health signals stop arriving.
  • Built usage/cost analytics views summarizing AI request volume, latency, and token/cost trends per application and per user, to support capacity planning and cost visibility.
  • Secured administrative access with Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on, restricted to a pre-approved list of administrators (no automatic account creation).
  • Deployed as an independent Azure App Service process alongside the main platform, using a Gunicorn/WSGI production server.

Machine Diagnostics Intelligence Assistant

An AI-powered assistant that helps technicians and support engineers quickly diagnose equipment issues by asking questions in plain language instead of manually searching through historical service/case records.

  • A technician types a question about a machine issue (symptoms, fault codes, or a specific model).
  • The system searches a large repository of historical diagnostic/case data for the most relevant past cases and information.
  • It combines semantic search (understanding meaning/context) with traditional keyword search, so relevant results surface even when the wording doesn't exactly match.
  • An AI model reads the most relevant matches and writes a clear, human-readable answer/summary — citing what it found rather than inventing information.
  • Built-in quality checks continuously validate that answers stay accurate and grounded in real data, rather than fabricated.

"Built an AI diagnostics assistant that lets technicians query historical equipment case data in natural language, using a hybrid search engine (semantic + keyword) and automated accuracy testing to ensure reliable, fact-based responses."

Volvo Product Intelligence Assistant

An AI-powered assistant that lets internal users ask natural-language questions about product specifications and get instant, accurate answers instead of manually paging through lengthy product documentation.

  • Product documentation is processed into an internal knowledge base, with special handling to accurately capture tabular data (specs, dimensions, capacities) that's typically harder for AI to read correctly than plain text.
  • User questions are matched against the knowledge base using a combination of semantic search and keyword matching, then ranked before being handed to an AI model.
  • The AI model generates a concise, accurate answer grounded in the retrieved content.
  • Safeguards prevent the assistant from responding with unsupported information when the requested data isn't available.

"Built an AI-powered product knowledge assistant that extracts structured data from technical documentation and answers natural-language product specification queries using semantic search and ranking algorithms."
